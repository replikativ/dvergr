# Waitrose

Waitrose is the supermarket chain of the [John Lewis Partnership](john-lewis-partnership.md), part of it in every snapshot from 2010 to 2025; for a time it was branded Waitrose & Partners ([2010 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2010.md), [2019 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2019.md), [2025 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2025.md)).
