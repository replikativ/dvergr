# John Lewis Partnership

The John Lewis Partnership was founded in London in 1929 by [John Spedan Lewis](john-spedan-lewis.md); the 2025 snapshot dates it to 23 April 1929 ([2025 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2025.md)). The oldest snapshot gives 1920 instead, which every later revision contradicts ([2010 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2010.md)).

It runs the John Lewis department stores and the [Waitrose](waitrose.md) supermarkets, and today also financial services and a build to rent business. A trust holds the company for its employees, who are called Partners ([2025 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2025.md)). Its legal form has changed: private in 2010 and 2013, a partnership in 2016 and 2019, and since then a public limited company, John Lewis Partnership plc ([2022 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2022.md)); it was registered as The John Lewis Partnership, Limited until 1982 ([2025 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2025.md)). Its head office is in Pimlico, in the City of Westminster ([2025 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2025.md)).

## Leadership

[Jason Tarry](jason-tarry.md) is the Chairman, Rita Clifton the Deputy Chairman and Nish Kankiwala the CEO ([2025 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2025.md)). Earlier chairmen: Charlie Mayfield, chairman in the 2010, 2013, 2016 and 2019 snapshots ([2019 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2019.md)), then Dame Sharon White, chairman in the 2022 snapshot ([2022 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2022.md)).

## Employees and revenue

About 74,000 people worked for it in 2024 ([2025 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2025.md)). The headcount was 68,430 in 2008 ([2010 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2010.md)), 84,700 in 2013 ([2013 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2013.md)), a peak of 88,700 in 2015 ([2016 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2016.md)), 83,000 in the 2019 snapshot ([2019 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2019.md)) and, in the 2022 snapshot, 80,000 for 2022 or around 80,800 for 2020 ([2022 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2022.md)).

Revenue in FY2023/24 was £12.426 billion, and operating income £147 million ([2025 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2025.md)). Earlier figures: £7.42 billion in 2009-2010 ([2010 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2010.md)), £9.5 billion in 2013 ([2013 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2013.md)), £10.2 billion in 2018 ([2019 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2019.md)) and £10.15 billion in 2020 ([2022 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2022.md)).

## Ocado

For years the Partnership supplied Waitrose and John Lewis own-brand goods to the Ocado web supermarket. That arrangement ended in September 2020, when Marks & Spencer took over with a £750 million contract with Ocado ([2022 snapshot](../docs/john-lewis-2022.md)).
