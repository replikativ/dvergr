# Iñigo Ucín

Iñigo Ucín is president of the General Council of the [Mondragon Corporation](mondragon-corporation.md);
the sources name him in that role from 2019 on ([2025 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2025.md)).

Earlier heads of the federation were Jose María Aldecoa, chairman in 2010
([2010 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2010.md)), Txema Gisasola, chairman in 2013
([2013 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2013.md)), and Javier Sotil, president of the General Council in 2016
([2016 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2016.md)).
