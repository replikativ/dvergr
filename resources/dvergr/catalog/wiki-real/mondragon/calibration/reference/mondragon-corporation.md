# Mondragon Corporation

The Mondragon Corporation is a federation of worker cooperatives in the Basque region of Spain. It was
founded on 14 April 1956 in the town of Mondragón by [José María Arizmendiarrieta](jose-maria-arizmendiarrieta.md)
and a group of students of the technical college he had set up; its first product was paraffin heaters
([2025 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2025.md)).

It groups 257 companies and organisations in four areas: finance, industry, retail and knowledge
([2022 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2022.md)). Its cooperatives follow the Statement on the Co-operative
Identity of the International Co-operative Alliance, and it is led by [Iñigo Ucín](inigo-ucin.md),
president of the General Council ([2025 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2025.md)).

## Employees

In 2024 it had over 70,000 workers: 30,660 in the Basque Country, 29,340 elsewhere in Spain and about
10,000 abroad ([2025 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2025.md)). In 2019 it employed 81,507 people
([2022 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2022.md)). Earlier counts were 92,773 at the end of 2008
([2010 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2010.md)); for 2012 the same revision gives 80,321 in its summary
and 83,321 in its text ([2013 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2013.md)); and 74,335 in 2015
([2016 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2016.md)).

## Finances

The most recent revenue in the sources is €12,110 million for 2015, with assets of €24,725 million for
2014 ([2016 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2016.md)). Revenue had been 16.8 billion € in 2008
([2010 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2010.md)) and 14,832 million € in 2011
([2013 snapshot](../docs/mondragon-2013.md)).
