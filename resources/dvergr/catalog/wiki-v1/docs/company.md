# Tessellate Instruments

Tessellate Instruments is a maker of seismic measurement equipment. It was founded in
1987 in Harrowgate Bay by the geophysicist Ines Varga and the electrical engineer
Tomas Oduya, who met while repairing survey equipment for a regional observatory.

The company started in a rented boathouse and built its first instruments by hand.
In 2004 it moved its headquarters from Harrowgate Bay to Calder Point, where it built a
dedicated assembly hall. Tessellate remains privately held.
