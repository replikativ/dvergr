# Kessel Works

Kessel Works is the cooperative's treatment plant on the Tallow River. It was known as the
Eastbank plant until it was renamed in 2015 after [Jonas Kessel](jonas-kessel.md), the
engineer who designed it in 1958 ([press release](../docs/press-release-rename.md)).

After its 2021 upgrade it treats 11 million litres a day
([annual report 2022](../docs/annual-report-2022.md)); in 2010 it treated 9 million
([annual report 2010](../docs/annual-report-2010.md)). [Mira Szabo](mira-szabo.md) ran its
operations before becoming general manager. It supplies the
[Morrow Valley Water Cooperative](morrow-valley-water-cooperative.md); a faulty sensor there
caused the [2018 boil-water notice](boil-water-notice-2018.md)
([incident report](../docs/incident-2018.md)).
