# Mira Szabo

Mira Szabo has been general manager of the
[Morrow Valley Water Cooperative](morrow-valley-water-cooperative.md) since 1 April 2019.
Before that she was head of operations at [Kessel Works](kessel-works.md) from 2012
([board minutes 2019](../docs/board-minutes-2019.md)).

She signed the partnership with the [Tallow River Trust](tallow-river-trust.md)
([annual report 2022](../docs/annual-report-2022.md)).
