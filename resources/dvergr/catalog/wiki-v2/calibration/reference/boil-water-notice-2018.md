# 2018 boil-water notice

On 3 May 2018 a sensor at [Kessel Works](kessel-works.md) reported high turbidity and the
cooperative issued a boil-water notice for the valley. Laboratory tests found no
contamination: the sensor was faulty. The notice was lifted on 17 May 2018, after 14 days
([incident report](../docs/incident-2018.md)).
