# Rowan Achebe

Rowan Achebe was appointed general manager of the
[Morrow Valley Water Cooperative](morrow-valley-water-cooperative.md) in 2008, succeeding
[Ada Lindqvist](ada-lindqvist.md) ([annual report 2010](../docs/annual-report-2010.md)). In 2010
he presented the plan for the [Heron Ridge pipeline](heron-ridge-pipeline.md).

He retired in 2019 after eleven years and was succeeded by [Mira Szabo](mira-szabo.md)
([board minutes 2019](../docs/board-minutes-2019.md)).
