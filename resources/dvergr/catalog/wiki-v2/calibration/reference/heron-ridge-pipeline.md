# Heron Ridge pipeline

The Heron Ridge pipeline brings water 23 kilometres from the Heron Ridge reservoir
([newsletter 2012](../docs/newsletter-2012.md)). Planned by [Rowan Achebe](rowan-achebe.md), it
was expected to open in 2013, but a landslide on the route delayed it and it opened in
October 2014; it delivers 6 million litres a day
([annual report 2014](../docs/annual-report-2014.md)).
