# Morrow Valley Water Cooperative

The Morrow Valley Water Cooperative was founded on 11 April 1953 by forty-two farming
households to build and run a shared water supply ([charter](../docs/charter-1953.md)). An
unofficial community history blog gives 1951 instead, which the charter contradicts
([blog](../docs/blog-history.md)).

It is owned by its members: each member household has one vote, and the annual meeting elects
a board of seven, which appoints the general manager ([charter](../docs/charter-1953.md)). It
serves 5,050 member households ([annual report 2022](../docs/annual-report-2022.md)), up from
4,200 in 2010 ([annual report 2010](../docs/annual-report-2010.md)).

General managers: [Ada Lindqvist](ada-lindqvist.md), [Rowan Achebe](rowan-achebe.md) (2008 to
2019) and [Mira Szabo](mira-szabo.md) (since 2019) ([board minutes 2019](../docs/board-minutes-2019.md)).
Its water comes from
[Kessel Works](kessel-works.md) and the [Heron Ridge pipeline](heron-ridge-pipeline.md); it
works with the [Tallow River Trust](tallow-river-trust.md).
