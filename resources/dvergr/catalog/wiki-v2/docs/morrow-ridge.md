# Morrow Ridge Irrigation District raises fees

The Morrow Ridge Irrigation District, a separate body on the far side of the ridge, raised
its fees by 8 percent. Its director, Colm Hartigan, said the district's 310 farms need a new
pumping station at Lark Hollow.
