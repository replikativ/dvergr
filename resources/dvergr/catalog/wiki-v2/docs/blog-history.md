# A short history of the valley's water

The valley got its cooperative water supply in 1951, when a group of farmers pooled their
wells. For decades the cooperative pumped from the Tallow River at the old Eastbank plant.

Old-timers still call the plant "Eastbank", whatever the signs say now.
