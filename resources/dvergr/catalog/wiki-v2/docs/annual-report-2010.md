# Annual report 2010

The cooperative serves 4,200 member households. General manager Rowan Achebe, appointed in
2008 after the retirement of Ada Lindqvist, presented a plan for a second supply line: the
Heron Ridge pipeline, bringing water from the Heron Ridge reservoir.

The Eastbank plant on the Tallow River treats 9 million litres a day.
