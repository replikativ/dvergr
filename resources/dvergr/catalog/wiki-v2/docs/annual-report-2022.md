# Annual report 2022

The cooperative now serves 5,050 member households. Kessel Works treats 11 million litres a
day after its 2021 upgrade, and the Heron Ridge pipeline supplies up to 6 million litres a
day in summer.

General manager Mira Szabo signed a partnership with the Tallow River Trust to restore the
riverbank upstream of Kessel Works.
