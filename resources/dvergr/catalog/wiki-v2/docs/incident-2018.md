# Boil-water notice, May 2018

On 3 May 2018 a sensor at Kessel Works reported high turbidity and the cooperative issued a
boil-water notice for the whole valley. Laboratory tests found no contamination; the sensor
was faulty. The notice was lifted on 17 May 2018.

The cooperative replaced the sensor array and added a second, independent sensor line.
