# Charter of the Morrow Valley Water Cooperative

The Morrow Valley Water Cooperative is founded on 11 April 1953 by forty-two farming
households of the Morrow Valley, to build and run a shared water supply.

The cooperative is owned by its members. Each member household has one vote at the annual
meeting, which elects a board of seven. The board appoints a general manager.
