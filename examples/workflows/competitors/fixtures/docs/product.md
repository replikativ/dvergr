# Simmis

Simmis is an organizational control system for work shared by people and AI agents. It gives an
organization a durable state of what it knows, has decided, and is doing. People and agents can
work from that state, run separate attempts, and adopt selected results without turning every
intermediate action into official organizational reality. The product combines a working business
interface with the programmable infrastructure underneath it.

Simmis matters when several workers, human and AI, need the same organizational context, work
outlives a chat or model, attempts may conflict, or adoption requires an authorized decision.
